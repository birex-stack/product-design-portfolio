Magnitude

Magnitude is a 0–10 priority score for the case (offense). Higher values should be investigated first.

It is derived from three inputs (each also scored 0–10):

  • Severity — potential impact of the threat relative to the target.
  • Relevance — importance of the affected asset or network to the organization.
  • Credibility — how trustworthy the evidence and detection sources are.

The overall Magnitude blends these factors and can be influenced by related event volume, offense age, and asset context. It is not the same as a single alert’s severity tag.
